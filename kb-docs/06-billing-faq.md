# Frequently Asked Billing & Tax Questions

## Invoices & Receipts

### Where can I download my official invoices?
All PDF invoices and tax receipts are stored under **Organization Settings > Billing > Invoice History**. Owners and billing admins can download receipt PDFs at any time.

### Can I add custom billing details (VAT / Tax ID / Purchase Order)?
Yes. Before downloading your invoice, click **Billing Details > Edit Business Info**. You can specify:
- Legal Company Name
- Tax Identification Number / VAT ID
- Custom Billing Address & PO Number

## Taxes & International VAT

### Does Deskwise charge Sales Tax or Value Added Tax (VAT)?
Applicable sales tax, GST, or VAT is calculated automatically at checkout based on your organization's country and state address:
- **US Customers**: State sales tax applied where required by law.
- **EU & UK Businesses**: Reverse-charge mechanism applies if a valid EU/UK VAT ID is provided.
- **Rest of World**: Local indirect taxes applied automatically.

## Payment Methods & Currencies

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) via Stripe. Enterprise customers paying annually ($1,000+) can request invoice payment via ACH, SEPA, or Wire Transfer.

### What billing currencies are supported?
Subscripton prices are listed in USD ($). Payments from international bank cards will be converted automatically by your card issuing bank at current market exchange rates.
