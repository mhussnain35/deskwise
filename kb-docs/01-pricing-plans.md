# Deskwise Pricing & Subscription Plans

## Overview
Deskwise offers three subscription tiers tailored for teams of all sizes: Free, Pro, and Enterprise. All plans include core workspace features, SSL encryption, and community support.

## Subscription Tiers

### 1. Free Tier ($0 / month)
- **Included Seats**: 1 user seat.
- **AI Response Allowance**: 100 AI support credits per month.
- **Knowledge Base Storage**: Up to 5 documents or 50 MB total storage.
- **Support**: Standard email support with 48-hour response SLAs.
- **Best For**: Solopreneurs, open-source projects, and early-stage testing.

### 2. Pro Tier ($29 / seat / month)
- **Included Seats**: Up to 10 user seats.
- **AI Response Allowance**: 5,000 AI support credits per month. Additional credits available at $0.005 per query.
- **Knowledge Base Storage**: Up to 500 documents or 10 GB total storage.
- **Features**: Hybrid search vector retrieval, custom domain integration, and priority chat support (4-hour SLA).
- **Billing Option**: Billed monthly or annually at $24/seat/month (save 18%).

### 3. Enterprise Tier ($99 / seat / month)
- **Included Seats**: Unlimited user seats (minimum 5 seats required).
- **AI Response Allowance**: 50,000 AI support credits per month with dedicated high-throughput capacity.
- **Knowledge Base Storage**: Unlimited documents and vector storage.
- **Features**: SOC 2 Type II compliance reports, custom SSO (SAML/Okta/Azure AD), dedicated account manager, 99.9% uptime SLA, and custom RAG fine-tuning.

## AI Credits & Overages
- AI credits reset automatically on the 1st day of each billing cycle.
- Unused credits do not roll over to subsequent months.
- Pro and Enterprise accounts can enable automatic overage protection in **Billing Settings** to avoid service interruptions.
