# Cancellation & Account Termination Policy

## How to Cancel Your Subscription
You can cancel your Deskwise subscription at any time directly through the self-serve admin panel.

### Step-by-Step Self-Serve Cancellation
1. Log into your account as an **Owner** or **Billing Administrator**.
2. Go to **Organization Settings > Billing & Subscriptions**.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the active plan card and click **Cancel Subscription**.
4. Complete the brief 2-question feedback prompt and click **Confirm Cancellation**.
5. You will receive an immediate email confirmation with your cancellation effective date.

## What Happens After Cancellation

### Access Until Billing Cycle End
When you cancel, your account does not shut down immediately. You maintain full access to all tier features, vector databases, and team seats until the final day of your paid billing period.

### Account Status & Data Retention Policy
- **30-Day Grace Window**: Following account expiration, your knowledge base docs, vectors, and conversation logs are preserved for 30 calendar days.
- **Read-Only Mode**: During the 30-day grace period, your team can export data or reactivate the subscription without losing setup configurations.
- **Permanent Deletion**: On Day 31 post-expiration, all vector indices in Qdrant and customer records in Neon are permanently scrubbed in accordance with GDPR requirements.

## Pausing Subscription vs. Cancelling
If you only need to pause operations temporarily, Deskwise offers a **Pause Account** option for up to 3 months at $5/month, maintaining all document indices and settings without incurring full seat costs.
