# Upgrading, Downgrading & Proration Rules

## Upgrading Your Plan

### Immediate Feature Access
Upgrades from Free to Pro or Pro to Enterprise take effect **immediately**. You gain instant access to higher seat limits, increased AI credits, and advanced vector search capabilities.

### Proration Credit Calculation
When you upgrade mid-cycle:
- We calculate the unused portion of your current plan as a credit.
- We charge the pro-rated price of the new plan for the remainder of the billing cycle.
- Example: If you upgrade 15 days into a 30-day $29/mo plan to a $99/mo plan, you receive a $14.50 credit towards the remaining half-month charge ($49.50), resulting in an immediate charge of $35.00.

## Downgrading Your Plan

### Effective Date of Downgrade
To avoid service disruption, plan downgrades (e.g. Enterprise to Pro, or Pro to Free) take effect **at the end of your current billing cycle**.

### Seat & Resource Adjustments on Downgrade
Before downgrading to a lower tier:
- **Excess Seats**: If your team has 8 active users and you downgrade to Free (limit 1 seat), you must remove 7 users prior to the renewal date.
- **Document Limits**: Ensure your stored documents fit within the lower tier's limit (e.g., 5 docs for Free tier). Excess documents above the limit will be archived in read-only state.
