# Deskwise Refund & Return Policy

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
We want you to be completely satisfied with Deskwise. All new **Pro** and **Enterprise** annual and monthly subscriptions are eligible for a **100% full refund** within **14 calendar days** of the initial subscription purchase date.

## Eligibility Conditions for Refunds

### Eligible Refund Requests
- **Initial Purchases**: First-time subscriptions cancelled within 14 days of purchase.
- **Service Outages**: Pro-rated refunds for service downtime exceeding our guaranteed 99.9% uptime SLA (Enterprise customers).
- **Billing Errors**: Duplicate charges or technical billing glitches reported within 30 days of the invoice date.

### Non-Refundable Items
- **Mid-Cycle Cancellations**: Cancellations requested after the 14-day window are non-refundable. Your service will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.
- **Add-on AI Credits**: Purchased credit top-ups or overage charges are non-refundable once consumed.
- **Domain Registrations & Custom Setup Fees**: Third-party integration fees or custom enterprise deployment services.

## Pro-Rated Refund Calculations
For annual plans cancelled within the 14-day window:
- You will receive a full refund minus any consumed overage charges.
- For billing disputes approved after 14 days by customer support, refunds are calculated on a pro-rated monthly basis according to unused full months remaining.

## How to Request a Refund
1. Log into your Deskwise Admin Console.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing & Invoices**.
3. Click **Request Refund** next to the transaction receipt or submit a ticket directly to `billing@deskwise.io`.
4. Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within **5 to 10 business days** depending on your issuing bank.
